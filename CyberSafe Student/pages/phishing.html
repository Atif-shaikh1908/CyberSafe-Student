Phishing
Awareness

Learn how to recognize suspicious messages, emails and links before interacting with them.

🎣

Think before you click

Phishing messages may try to convince users to reveal information, open a suspicious link or take an unexpected action. Learning to identify warning signs can help students make safer decisions online.

Common Phishing Warning Signs

⚠️ Urgent Messages

Be careful when a message creates unusual urgency and asks you to act immediately.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Check links carefully before opening them, especially when they arrive unexpectedly.

👤 Unknown Senders

Treat unexpected messages from unknown senders with caution.

🔐 Requests for Information

Be cautious if an unexpected message asks for passwords, account information or other personal details.

🌐 Unusual Websites

Before entering information, check that the website address is the expected one.

📩 Unexpected Attachments

Avoid opening unexpected attachments until you can verify where they came from.

What Should You Do?

01

Stop

Don't rush to respond to an unexpected message or request.

02

Check

Examine the sender, message and website address carefully.

03

Verify

Use a trusted method to verify the request before taking action.

04

Report

If appropriate, report suspicious messages through the relevant service.
