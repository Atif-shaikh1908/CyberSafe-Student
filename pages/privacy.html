Awareness

Learn how to make thoughtful decisions about personal information when using websites, applications and online services.

🔒

Protect your personal information

Students share information across websites, applications and social platforms. Understanding what information is being shared and who may access it is an important part of online privacy.

Privacy Protection Practices

👤 Share Carefully

Think carefully before sharing personal information online or with unfamiliar services.

📱 Review App Permissions

Review the permissions requested by applications and consider whether they are appropriate.

⚙️ Check Privacy Settings

Review available privacy and account settings on websites and applications.

📸 Think Before Posting

Consider whether personal photos, information or other content should be shared publicly.

📍 Be Careful With Location

Review location-related permissions and avoid sharing location information unnecessarily.

🔐 Protect Account Information

Use appropriate account security practices and avoid sharing credentials with others.

A Simple Privacy Process

01

Think

Consider what information you are about to share.

02

Check

Check the privacy settings and permissions of the service or application.

03

Decide

Decide whether sharing the information is necessary for the activity.

04

Protect

Keep important account and personal information protected.
