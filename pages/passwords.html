Strong account security starts with good password practices. Learn simple ways to make your accounts safer.

🔐

Protect your online accounts

Students use many online accounts for education, communication, entertainment and other digital services. Password security is an important part of protecting these accounts.

Basic Password Safety Tips

🔑 Use Strong Passwords

Use passwords that are difficult for others to guess and avoid simple personal information.

🔄 Avoid Reusing Passwords

Using different passwords for important accounts can reduce the impact if one password becomes compromised.

🛡️ Use Multi-Factor Authentication

Where available, additional verification can provide another layer of account protection.

🚫 Don't Share Passwords

Keep your account credentials private and avoid sharing passwords with other people.

💻 Be Careful on Shared Devices

When using a shared computer, make sure you sign out of important accounts after finishing your session.

👀 Watch for Suspicious Requests

Be careful when a message or website asks for your password or account information unexpectedly.
