Browse safely and carefully

Students regularly use websites for education, communication, entertainment and online services. Checking websites and avoiding suspicious content can help reduce common online risks.

Safe Browsing Practices

🔎 Check the Website Address

Check the website address carefully before entering passwords or other information.

🔒 Look for Secure Connections

Pay attention to the browser's security indicators when visiting websites.

⚠️ Avoid Suspicious Websites

Be cautious with websites reached through unexpected messages, advertisements or unfamiliar links.

📥 Be Careful With Downloads

Download files and applications only from sources you can reasonably trust.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Keep your browser and device software updated when updates are available.

🧠 Think Before You Click

Take a moment to consider unexpected links, pop-ups and online requests before interacting with them.

A Simple Browsing Safety Process

01

Check

Check the website address and the context in which you received the link.

02

Verify

Verify that the website or download source is appropriate before continuing.

03

Browse

Use the website carefully and avoid unnecessary sharing of personal information.

04

Exit

Leave suspicious websites instead of continuing to interact with unexpected content.
